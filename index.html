Privacy Policy

Application: Vroom - RC Car Controller
Effective Date: October 2026
Last Updated: October 2026

Core Privacy Summary: Vroom does NOT collect, record, track, store, or transmit any personal data, location data, or device analytics. The app functions entirely locally on your device without internet connection.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiable Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account registrations.
  • No Location Tracking: GPS and location data are never accessed, monitored, or stored.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No tracking SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry tools are integrated.
  • No Advertisements: Zero advertising networks or user profiling.

2. Device Permissions Used

Vroom requests the minimum permissions required to communicate with Bluetooth robotics hardware:

Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN)

Allows the application to discover and pair with your RC car hardware (HC-05, HC-06, ESP32, Arduino) and send directional driving commands over standard Serial Port Profile (SPP).

Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Required exclusively by older Android operating systems (Android 11 and earlier) as an OS security requirement to perform Bluetooth device scans. Vroom does not access your geographical location. On Android 12+, modern runtime Bluetooth permissions are used without location access.

Vibration / Haptics

Provides physical tactile vibration feedback when pressing control buttons and shifting gears.

3. Children’s Privacy

Vroom complies with global child privacy regulations including COPPA and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We do not collect information from children or adults of any age.

4. Security

Because the app operates strictly offline between your smartphone and the RC car receiver, your communications are direct, local, and completely private.
